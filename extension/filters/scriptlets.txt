! Jev Blocker scriptlet rules.
!
! Syntax is uBlock Origin / Brave compatible, so rules can be lifted from the
! community lists unchanged and dropped in here.
!
!   domain1,domain2##+js(scriptlet-name, arg1 arg2, arg3)
!
! These exist because YouTube is the one site the network and cosmetic layers
! cannot help. Ads come from the same hosts as the video, and the ad data is
! handed to the player as part of the player response, so the only place to
! intervene is between the response arriving and the player reading it.
!
! The rules below remove the ad fields from that response. `json-prune` covers
! `JSON.parse` and `Response.json`; YouTube uses `fetch` for the player call, so
! the `-fetch-response` variant is the one that actually fires in practice. The
! XHR variant is kept because the player falls back to XHR on some surfaces.
!
! Compiled by tools/compile_scriptlets.py into extension/scriptlets/.

! --- YouTube -----------------------------------------------------------------
!
! Fields, and why each one is listed:
!   adPlacements             the pre-roll / mid-roll slots themselves
!   playerAds                player-level ad config
!   adSlots                  older slot list, still present on some surfaces
!   adParams                 targeting parameters handed to the ad server
!   adBreakHeartbeatParams   lets the client report ad playback back
!
! Removing them leaves the player with nothing to schedule. Playback fields
! (streamingData, videoDetails) are untouched, so video still plays.

youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com##+js(json-prune, adPlacements playerAds adSlots adParams adBreakHeartbeatParams playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.playerAds)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com##+js(json-prune-fetch-response, adPlacements playerAds adSlots adParams adBreakHeartbeatParams playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.playerAds)
youtube.com,youtube-nocookie.com##+js(json-prune-xhr-response, adPlacements playerAds adSlots adParams adBreakHeartbeatParams playerResponse.adPlacements playerResponse.playerAds)
